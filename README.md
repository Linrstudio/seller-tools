# Seller Tools

Free cross-platform seller fee & profit calculators for Amazon, Etsy, eBay, Shopify and TikTok Shop sellers.

Site: https://seller-tools.qingyinhui.com/

Compare what you actually keep from every sale — platform fees, fulfillment, ads, returns and shipping, all in one place. Plus a reverse pricing calculator: tell it the profit you want, it tells you what to charge on each platform.

Status: launching soon. Fee schedules are maintained against each platform's published 2026 rates.
Tile & Flooring sellers and flooring contractors can use the [Tile Quote & Margin Calculator](https://seller-tools.qingyinhui.com/tile-seller-tools/) to build job quotes and check material, waste, labor, delivery and margin assumptions before committing.
