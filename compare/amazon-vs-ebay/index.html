Amazon FBA vs eBay: which keeps more?

eBay's 13.6% looks close to Amazon's 15% referral — but Amazon adds fulfillment on top, while eBay's percentage secretly grows with your buyer's sales tax. Same product, both stacks, five prices.

Where each one bites

Amazon bites in the open: fulfillment, storage, placement, plan. eBay bites quietly: the FVF applies to the tax-inclusive total, so in high-tax states your effective rate creeps toward 15% while Amazon's stays put. For one-off, used, or long-tail inventory, eBay also skips the inbound-shipping-to-a-warehouse step entirely — you ship once, to the buyer.

The verdict pattern

For standardized products at volume, FBA's logistics usually repay their fees in conversion. For irregular inventory — the thing eBay was built for — Amazon's stack has nothing to attach to. The price bands above show the fee story; the inventory story is yours.
