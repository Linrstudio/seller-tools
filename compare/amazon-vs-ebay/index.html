Amazon FBA vs eBay: which keeps more?

eBay's 13.6% looks close to Amazon's 15% referral — but Amazon adds fulfillment on top, while eBay's percentage secretly grows with your buyer's sales tax. Same product, both stacks, five prices.

Where each one bites

Amazon bites in the open: fulfillment, storage, placement, plan. eBay bites quietly: the FVF applies to the tax-inclusive total, so in high-tax states your effective rate creeps toward 15% while Amazon's stays put. For one-off, used, or long-tail inventory, eBay also skips the inbound-shipping-to-a-warehouse step entirely — you ship once, to the buyer.

The verdict pattern

For standardized products at volume, FBA's logistics usually repay their fees in conversion. For irregular inventory — the thing eBay was built for — Amazon's stack has nothing to attach to. The price bands above show the fee story; the inventory story is yours.

Bottom line: is eBay cheaper than Amazon?

On fees alone, yes — and not by a rounding error. Under the same canonical profile (product cost 35% of price, inbound $1.20, packaging $0.30, self-shipping $4.80 where relevant, ads at 10% of price, 5% returns, 300 units/month), eBay keeps more per sale at every price in the table above. FBA's argument was never “cheaper fees”; it is Prime delivery, Buy Box conversion, and a buyer pool several times larger. If your product sells on its own merits and you can ship it yourself for a few dollars, eBay's fee stack wins. If you need Amazon's traffic to move volume at all, FBA's higher fees are rent on demand that already exists — pay it knowingly.

FAQ

eBay vs Amazon fees: which is cheaper? eBay, on fees alone. On a $50 sale with identical costs, Amazon FBA charges about $12.53 in platform fees while eBay charges $7.20 — eBay keeps about $1.50 more per sale.

What are eBay vs Amazon selling fees on a $50 item? Amazon FBA: 15% referral ($7.50) plus fulfillment ($4.47), the 3.5% fuel surcharge, placement and plan share — about $12.53 total. eBay: 13.6% final value fee ($6.80) plus a $0.40 per-order fee — $7.20 total. Same product, same other costs: eBay nets about $12.81 per sale versus $12.29 on FBA.

Is eBay cheaper than Amazon for sellers? Yes on fees — but FBA buys Prime conversion and Amazon's larger buyer pool, which often matters more than a dollar or two per sale.

Do eBay fees include sales tax? Yes. eBay's final value fee is charged on the total amount of the sale, including shipping and the sales tax eBay collects from the buyer. On a $100 sale at 8% tax, you pay fees on $108, not $100.

Amazon FBA vs eBay for books and clothing? For a $50 book, FBA nets $10.49 versus eBay's $11.96. For $50 clothing, FBA nets $11.29 versus eBay's $12.81. See the category table above.
