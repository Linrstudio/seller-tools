Amazon FBA vs Shopify: which keeps more?

Shopify's processing fee is a tenth of Amazon's stack. The catch is that Amazon's stack includes the customers. This comparison holds ad spend equal at 10% — read the verdict, then ask what your real Shopify ad cost is.

Renting demand vs owning it

Amazon's fees are rent on demand that already exists. Shopify's low fees assume you manufacture demand yourself — with ads, content, or an audience. At the canonical 10% ad rate Shopify wins almost everywhere; at the 25–40% blended ad cost many new stores actually pay, the gap narrows or inverts. The Shopify calculator lets you raise the ad line until it's honest.

The fee that compounds

A Shopify customer is yours — email, retargeting, repeat orders at ~3%. An Amazon customer is Amazon's. Over a product line's life, that difference routinely outweighs the per-sale fee gap shown above.
