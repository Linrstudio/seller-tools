The commission is the fee

At the canonical 10% affiliate rate, TikTok's distribution costs 16% before delivery — already past Amazon's referral, though still short of the full FBA stack. Push the commission to 20% (a normal launch offer) and TikTok's take approaches Amazon's, with you still doing the shipping. The TikTok calculator lets you set your real offered rate.
