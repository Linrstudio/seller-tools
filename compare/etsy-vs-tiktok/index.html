Etsy vs TikTok Shop: which keeps more?

A marketplace that brings the buyer against a feed that makes the buyer: Etsy's stacked small fees versus TikTok's low referral rate — with one catch that flips the result.

Who wins at each price?

Canonical profile: product cost 35% of price, inbound $1.20, packaging $0.30, self-shipping $4.80 where relevant, ads 10% of price, returns 5%, 300 units/month, TikTok affiliate 10%. Change the real inputs on each platform's own calculator page.

The affiliate is the whole story

TikTok's 6% referral rate looks like the cheapest marketplace fee on this site — until the affiliate program takes its 10% of the sale price on top. With affiliate on (the canonical profile), Etsy wins at every price in the bands below, by roughly $2–$17 per sale. Turn affiliate off in the TikTok calculator and the ordering reverses: TikTok's bare 6% beats Etsy's ~11% stack. So the real question is never “Etsy or TikTok” — it is “do you need creators to sell this for you?”

Different buyers, different products

Etsy buyers search with intent for handmade, vintage and personalized goods; TikTok buyers discover through video and impulse. A product that needs explaining sells on Etsy; a product that demos in fifteen seconds sells on TikTok. The fee gap is real but smaller than the conversion gap — pick the room where your product already makes sense, then check the table.
