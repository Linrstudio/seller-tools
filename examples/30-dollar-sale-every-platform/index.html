Why Shopify “wins” — and when it doesn't

Shopify takes the least because it provides the least: no marketplace traffic (the 10% ad assumption is doing heavy lifting, and real Shopify ad costs are often higher), no fulfillment network, no built-in trust. Amazon FBA's $9+ fee stack buys all three. The honest reading of this table is not “sell on Shopify” — it's “know what each platform's cut purchases, and price for the one you're on.”
