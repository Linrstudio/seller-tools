How much does eBay take from a $50.00 sale?

Now add the buyer's sales tax

If the buyer paid 8% sales tax, eBay charges its FVF on $54.00 instead of $50.00 — fees rise to $7.74 and your profit falls to $15.32, even though the tax money never touches your account.

Assumptions

Most-categories item, no store, you pay $6.50 shipping, product cost 35% of price, inbound $1.50, packaging $0.40, 4% return rate, no promoted listing.
