Why the headline fee is never the real fee

Every marketplace advertises one friendly number — Etsy says 6.5%, TikTok says 6%, eBay says ~13%. None of them is what leaves your account. Etsy adds a $0.20 listing fee and payment processing on the total including shipping, which takes a typical US order to about 11%. Amazon stacks referral, fulfillment, storage, plan fees and, since April 2026, a 3.5% fuel surcharge on fulfillment. eBay charges its final value fee on the total including the sales tax the buyer paid. Shopify looks free of marketplace fees until you divide a $39–$399 monthly plan by a small order count.

That's why this tool works backwards from your settlement, not forwards from a fee table: platform fees first, then the costs sellers forget — inbound freight, packaging, ad spend as a share of price, and the expected loss from returns (we model a return as losing the product cost plus the outbound delivery, with percentage fees refunded).
