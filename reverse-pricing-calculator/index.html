Why price backwards?

Percentage fees scale with price, fixed fees don't, and some fees (Amazon's clothing tiers, Etsy's Offsite Ads) jump at thresholds. That means “cost + 40%” lands somewhere different on every platform. Solving backwards — the way this tool does, by testing prices against the full fee engine until your target is hit to the cent — is the only way the number on your listing and the number in your bank account agree.

Use it before you order inventory, not after: if the required price on your best platform is above what the market pays, the product is wrong, not the price. Change the product, the bundle, or the platform — the calculator will tell you within a dollar whether the new idea clears your floor.

Want the forward view instead? The comparison calculator takes one price and shows profit everywhere; each platform page (Amazon, Etsy, eBay, TikTok, Shopify) goes line by line.
