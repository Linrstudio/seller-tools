TikTok Shop's real fee stack

Referral fee — 6% (most categories). Charged on the order total including shipping you charge, net of discounts and excluding buyer-paid tax. Jewelry and pre-owned sit at 5%. Rates were introduced per category in 2024–2025; if your Seller Center shows a different rate for your category, type it in — the field is editable on purpose.

Creator affiliate commission — the big one. Most TikTok Shop volume runs through creators, and the commission you offer (typically 5–20%, sometimes 30%+ for launches) usually exceeds the referral fee two or three times over. Any “TikTok only takes 6%” comparison quietly assumes this is zero.

Delivery. Self-shipping means your postage label; FBT (Fulfilled by TikTok) bundles warehousing and delivery into a per-order fee that shows up as a deduction. Either way, it's real money per order — put your actual number in.

Refund administration fee. Refund an order and TikTok keeps 20% of that order's referral fee (capped at $5 per item). Small per order, but TikTok return rates in fashion-adjacent categories are not small — we model it as an expected cost from your return rate.

Co-funded promotions. Campaign discounts TikTok part-funds change your realized price rather than your fees; model them by lowering the price input.

FAQ

Why do sellers say TikTok is “cheaper than Amazon”? Because 6% < 15%. Add a 12% affiliate commission and $4.60 delivery and the same $25 product keeps less on TikTok than the headline suggests — the honest comparison is exactly what the six-platform tool on our homepage does.

What commission should I offer creators? Work backwards from this calculator: set the commission so your per-order profit still clears your floor. If a 15% commission makes the order unprofitable, no volume of creator sales fixes that — it scales the loss.
