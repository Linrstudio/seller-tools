TikTok Shop's real fee stack

Referral fee — 6% (most categories). Charged on the order total including shipping you charge, net of discounts and excluding buyer-paid tax. Jewelry and pre-owned sit at 5%. Two August 2026 trade reports say the standard US rate rose to 8% on most non-food categories, while TikTok's own page still says 6% — check your Seller Center statement and type in your actual rate; the field is editable on purpose.

Creator affiliate commission — the big one. Most TikTok Shop volume runs through creators, and the commission you offer usually exceeds the referral fee two or three times over. You set the rate per product, anywhere from 1% to 80%; everyday categories typically land at 10–20%, open collaborations at 10–15%, with a reported 2026 US average around 13%. TikTok calculates it on revenue after refunds, so a returned order claws the commission back. Any “TikTok only takes 6%” comparison quietly assumes this is zero.

Delivery. Self-shipping means your postage label; FBT (Fulfilled by TikTok) bundles warehousing and delivery into a per-order fee that shows up as a deduction. Either way, it's real money per order — put your actual number in.

Refund administration fee. Refund an order and TikTok keeps 20% of that order's referral fee (capped at $5 per item). Small per order, but TikTok return rates in fashion-adjacent categories are not small — we model it as an expected cost from your return rate.

Co-funded promotions. Campaign discounts TikTok part-funds change your realized price rather than your fees; model them by lowering the price input.

TikTok Shop affiliate commission calculator

The calculator above is also an affiliate commission calculator: set the creator commission field and the per-order breakdown shows exactly what the commission costs you, line by line, next to the referral fee. The formula TikTok uses is (revenue − refunds) × commission rate, on the price the customer actually paid.

Worked example: a $24.99 product at the default 12% commission pays the creator $3.00 per order. Add the 6% referral fee ($1.50) and the platform-plus-creator take is $4.50 before delivery — 18% of the price, three times the “6%” headline. Change the rate to see what a 10%, 15% or 20% offer does to your margin before you publish it in the Affiliate Center.

One practical note: cutting a rate only reaches creators already promoting the product after about 30 days, so a rate you set today is effectively locked for a month. Set it with the calculator, not by gut feel.

FAQ

How do I calculate TikTok Shop affiliate commission? (Revenue − refunds) × the rate you set. On a $24.99 order at 12%, that's $3.00 to the creator. The calculator above runs the same formula on your own numbers, next to the referral fee, so you see the full take per order.

Why do sellers say TikTok is “cheaper than Amazon”? Because 6% < 15%. Add a 12% affiliate commission and $4.60 delivery and the same $25 product keeps less on TikTok than the headline suggests — the honest comparison is exactly what the six-platform tool on our homepage does.

What commission should I offer creators? Work backwards from this calculator: set the commission so your per-order profit still clears your floor. If a 15% commission makes the order unprofitable, no volume of creator sales fixes that — it scales the loss.
